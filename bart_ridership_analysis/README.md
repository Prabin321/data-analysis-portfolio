# Winter Sports Interest Trend Analysis | Excel & Google Trends

## Overview
Analyzed Google Trends data for multiple winter sports to identify recurring interest patterns, seasonality, and the impact of global events on audience engagement.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Time-Series Visualization, Charts, Trend Analysis)
- Google Trends (Data source)

## Dataset
- Google Trends interest-over-time data for multiple winter sports
- Multi-year time series capturing seasonal and event-driven spikes

## Key Findings
- **Recurring 4-year spikes** tied directly to global sporting events (Winter Olympics cycles)
- Strong seasonal baseline patterns with predictable annual peaks in winter months
- Significant variation between sports: some show only event-driven spikes while others maintain consistent baseline interest
- Distinct difference between **baseline engagement** vs **event-driven engagement** identified across all sports analyzed

## Methodology
1. Extracted Google Trends data for multiple winter sports over a multi-year period
2. Built time-series visualizations to map interest patterns and seasonality
3. Compared sports side-by-side to distinguish consistent performers from event-dependent ones
4. Identified the 4-year Olympic cycle as the primary driver of interest spikes

## Recommendations
- **Seasonal marketing**: Launch campaigns 6–8 weeks before peak winter season to capture early interest
- **Event-based content**: Plan major content pushes around Olympic cycles for maximum audience reach
- **Year-round engagement**: Focus on sports with consistent baseline interest for always-on strategies
- **Audience targeting**: Differentiate messaging for casual seasonal fans vs. dedicated year-round followers

## Business Impact
Findings enable more accurate audience forecasting, improved campaign timing, and data-driven content strategies aligned with proven interest cycles.
